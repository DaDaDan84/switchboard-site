Kids don't need a smartphone to call a friend

They need a phone. Switchboard gives them the good part of growing up with a phone — calling your friends, being trusted with your own line — and none of the part that keeps parents up at night.

1

Bring a phone

Thrift a cool vintage phone with your kid, or use any cheap corded one. It's their phone — they pick it.

2

Plug in the box

Our little connector joins your home WiFi and quietly links the phone to the Switchboard network. Setup takes minutes.

3

Dial a friend

Three digits and it rings — real dial tone and all. Only the friends you approve. Nobody else can get through.

Made for the way parents actually worry

Every choice here is about giving kids independence without giving them the internet.

No scrolling, ever

It's a telephone. There's nothing to scroll, no browser, no apps, no games, no social media.

A closed circle

Your kid can only reach friends you've approved — a shared line among your community, not the open phone network. No robocalls, no strangers.

You're the operator

Parents manage the approved list and set quiet hours, so late-night chatting isn't a thing.

Real, and a little magic

Genuine dial tones, a busy signal. It feels like a real phone because it is one.

Numbers little ones can remember

Three digits, not ten. It's a private numbering plan for your own community — a school, a street, a friend group — and you decide who's 101 and who's 102.
